Website Design

What we build and what it costs you in time

A website is the one employee that works every night, every weekend, and every holiday without ever calling in sick, which makes it strange how many DFW businesses leave theirs broken or never hire one at all. We are Wing Digital, a Plano shop that builds sites for home service businesses and local startups, and we do it on a model built for owners instead of marketing departments: one flat monthly rate once it is live, with everything handled.

What does the website service actually include?

Everything that sits between "I need a website" and a live site that wins work. That means the design, every word of the copy, your real photos placed where they carry weight, the domain registered in your name, the hosting set up and maintained, and a contact form wired straight to your inbox so a lead never falls through a crack.

You show up with your business information and we do the rest. We write the copy ourselves rather than handing you a template with blanks to fill, because the words are what turn a visitor into a phone call, and "insert text here" has never booked a single job. When the site launches, it is genuinely finished, not a shell you are quietly expected to complete on your own time.

How fast can my site go live?

Two weeks from the first call, as long as we have your information and your photos in hand. We hold that timeline on purpose, because every week a business runs without a real website is a week of customers quietly finding a competitor who does have one.

Bigger builds, the ones with a page for every city you serve or extra features like galleries and booking, get a clear timeline and an exact quote up front on our free consultation, instead of an optimistic guess. Either way you know the launch date before we start, and we hit it. Speed is not a gimmick here, it is the difference between capturing this season and waiting for the next one.

What happens after the site launches?

This is where the monthly model earns its keep, and where most websites quietly fall apart. A site that launches and is then abandoned begins aging the same day: prices drift out of date, a service you dropped is still listed, photos go stale, and Google slowly registers that nobody is home. Your monthly rate covers ongoing updates and support, so changing a price or swapping a photo is a quick message to us, never an hourly invoice.

On-page SEO is built into the site from day one, not bolted on later at a premium, because a beautiful site that Google cannot read is just an expensive brochure nobody sees. We can also wire the form straight into our lead generation system, so every lead the site captures gets an answer in minutes. Maintenance is not an upsell in our world. It is the whole point of paying monthly instead of once.

What if I already have a website?

We audit it for free on the consultation and give you the honest read. Sometimes a site genuinely needs a full rebuild, sometimes it just needs a refresh and some SEO, and when the cheaper path gets you the same result, that is the path we recommend.

Telling an owner their website is fine is a terrible sales strategy, and we do it anyway, because the owners who really do need a rebuild tend to remember the company that was straight with them. You will never get talked into a project you do not need on a call with us, and you can see the kind of sites this produces on our work page.
