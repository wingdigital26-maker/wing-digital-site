Why the wait pays06 / 07

Anyone promising you the top of page one in two weeks is guessing with your money, and we would rather give you the honest timeline than win you with a number we would have to take back.

The pages we publish and the reviews we build in the spring are still bringing calls in the fall.

The targets07 / 07

Which keywords do you actually target?

The ones your customers type in the last hour before they hire someone: your service plus their city, "near me" searches, and the cost and how-to questions specific to your trade. Those are the searches with real buying intent, and they are where a local business should spend its effort first.

We chase terms with genuine intent and weak competition before the vanity terms, because ranking for a word nobody buys on is decoration. The full method is written up in our local SEO playbook, and the flat monthly rate that covers it is quoted on the free consultation.

Working with the site you already have

If your current site is solid we build the SEO on top of it and tell you exactly that on the free call, because there is no reason to sell you a rebuild you do not need. Google checks the website behind your profile to confirm what you do and where, so a strong site makes every other piece work harder.

When the site genuinely is the problem, a thin one-pager that gives Google nothing to verify, we show you why on the call. In that case the web development and the SEO become one project instead of two separate bills, and the leads it produces land in the CRM rather than a text thread.
