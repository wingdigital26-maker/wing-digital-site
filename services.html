Services

Everything your business needs to get found and booked.

Four services. One team. All handled.

Wing Digital does one job: we get DFW businesses found by the customers already looking for them. Everything we sell serves that job. A website that turns visitors into calls. Local SEO that puts you on page one when someone searches your service and city. Social media that makes you look alive and trustworthy when they check up on you before calling.

Most owners come to us with some version of the same problem. The work is good, the referrals are steady, but the internet has no idea they exist. A competitor with worse reviews and higher prices is getting the calls because they show up first. That is not a talent problem. It is a visibility problem, and visibility is a system you can build.

How the four services work together

The website is the foundation. It is where every search, every ad, and every referral eventually lands, and it either turns that visit into a phone call or it quietly loses one. We build sites that load fast, read clearly, and ask for the call, and we have them live in two weeks.

Local SEO is the engine on top of it. Google decides who shows up when a Plano homeowner searches for your trade, and it decides based on your Google Business Profile, your content, your reviews, and the site behind it all. Our local SEO service works all four every single month, because rankings are earned monthly, not installed once.

Social media is the trust layer. Nobody hires a plumber off Instagram, but plenty of people check the Instagram before they call, and a page that went quiet in March reads like a business that might not pick up. Our social service keeps you looking active everywhere customers check, from one fifteen-minute check-in a month.

Then there is the part most agencies leave out. Email and lead generation covers both ends of the pipeline: targeted outreach that fills the gaps when referrals go quiet, and automated follow-up that answers every lead in minutes, keeps going for weeks, and requests a review after each finished job. Speed and persistence win work that price alone does not.

What working with us looks like

It starts with a free fifteen-minute call. You tell us about the business, we tell you honestly what you need, and just as important, what you do not. If your current site is fine, we say so. If all you need is the SEO, we quote just that. Then we build, you approve, and the monthly work starts compounding. One flat rate, month to month, one person who knows your business. You can see the systems doing the work on our work page.
