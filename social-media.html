Social Media

What we post and how little you have to do

Nobody hires a plumber because of a great Instagram post, but plenty of people quietly check the Instagram before they call, and a page that went silent back in March reads like a business that might not even pick up the phone anymore. We are Wing Digital, and we keep DFW businesses looking active and trustworthy online without the owner ever touching a keyboard.

What does the social media service include?

We plan, write, design, and publish your posts across Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business every month. The graphics are built to match your website and your logo, so a customer who bounces from your Instagram to your site to your Google profile sees one consistent business instead of three that happen to share a name.

Google Business posts are always part of the mix, because they are the most overlooked channel in all of local marketing. Fresh posts on your profile keep the very listing that ranks you looking active, which quietly helps your local SEO at the same time it reaches the people already looking at you.

How much of my time does this take?

Fifteen minutes a month. One short check-in where you tell us about recent jobs, hand over a few phone photos, and mention anything coming up. We take that and turn it into a full month of content, written and scheduled.

If you like to approve posts before they go live, we send a simple monthly calendar you can green-light with one tap. If you would genuinely rather never think about it again, that works too. The point of the service is to give you a real presence without adding a single recurring task to your week.

What about comments and messages?

Anything that looks like a lead gets flagged to you the same day, because a hot message that sits unanswered for a week goes cold and hires whoever replied first. If you want that handled automatically, that is what our lead generation service handles automatically. That fast hand-off is the part that actually protects revenue, so we treat it as the priority, not an afterthought.

The rest, the likes and the passing emoji comments, we simply fold into your monthly report so you can see the activity without being interrupted by it. You hear about the things that matter and stay out of the noise that does not.

Where do the photos come from?

Your real jobs, plus branded graphics we design to fill the gaps. Your work is always the strongest material, and a slightly imperfect photo of your actual crew on an actual job will out-perform a flawless stock photo of nobody every time, because customers can smell a stock photo from a mile away.

Generic stock is how every business in a category ends up posting the same smiling model in the same clean hard hat, and it quietly tells people you are interchangeable with everyone else. Social is one piece of the flat monthly plan, quoted on the free consultation. We would rather show the real thing, because the real thing is what makes you specific.
