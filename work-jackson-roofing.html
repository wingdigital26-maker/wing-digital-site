Jackson Roofing

Website, local SEO, and content for a North Texas roofer

Jackson Roofing is a family roofing company in Plano that has been on North Texas roofs since 2000. Great reputation, steady referrals, and almost no online presence to match. The job was to build the whole growth system: a site that earns trust fast, and the local SEO and content engine that gets them found when a homeowner searches after a storm.

What we designed

A bold, high-contrast website that looks as sturdy as the work. The homepage leads with "Expert Roofing For Homes And Businesses," a real photo of the branded truck on a job, and a single clear action: explore the services and book a free inspection. Residential and commercial menus split the two audiences cleanly so nobody has to hunt.

The copy is written in the owner's plain, honest voice, the same voice that wins jobs in person. No hype, no pressure, just a straight explanation of the work and an easy way to get a free look at your roof.

What we built

Beyond the main site, we build a dedicated landing page for each city they serve, so a search for roof repair in a specific town lands on a page about that town. On top of that runs a steady stream of blog content aimed at the exact questions homeowners type, cost questions, storm-damage questions, repair-or-replace questions, all built on our local SEO system.

Every page carries the schema and on-page structure Google needs to rank a local business, and the whole thing is designed to turn a search into a booked free inspection.

The result

A roofing company that finally looks online the way it performs in person, with a site and a content engine working every day to put it in front of North Texas homeowners the moment they need a roofer.
