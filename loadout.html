What happens after you pick

  1. You reply with your letters (or "your call" and I take the marked picks).
  2. I build the home page first to the locked personality so you can judge the real thing, not a board.
  3. Three judges review it in parallel: brief fidelity, craft precision at 375px and 1440px, and an adversarial slop check. They loop until it passes or four rounds are up.
  4. Then it rolls across all 17 pages and the rules get written down in design-dna.md so future pages inherit the look instead of re-rolling the dice.
