The service01 - What we do

When a Plano homeowner needs a plumber, they do not scroll to page two of Google, they call one of the first names they see. Local SEO is the ongoing work of being one of those names when your customers search, and it is a system run month after month, not a switch you flip once. We are Wing Digital, and we run that system for DFW businesses so the phone keeps ringing without you thinking about it.

What does the local SEO service include?

Four systems working together, every month. Your Google Business Profile managed and posted to like the storefront it actually is. Two keyword-targeted blog posts written and published, aimed at the questions your customers ask right before they hire. City landing pages built one real page at a time for the towns you serve. And a monthly ranking report written in plain English, so you can see exactly which searches you climbed and what we are working on next.

No jargon, no forty-page PDF designed to look busy, and nothing that quietly auto-publishes without a human checking it first. You always know what went out under your name and why it went out.

How long until local SEO actually works?

Real movement usually shows in sixty to ninety days, with meaningful, steady lead flow by month four to six. Anyone promising you the top of page one in two weeks is guessing with your money, and we would rather tell you the honest timeline than win you with a number we would have to take back later.

The trade-off for that patience is that SEO compounds in a way ads never can. The pages we publish and the reviews we build in the spring are still bringing you calls in the fall, because you are building an asset that keeps working, not renting attention that stops the moment you stop paying.

Do I need a new website for SEO to work?

Not necessarily. If your current site is solid, we build the SEO on top of it and tell you exactly that on the free call, because there is no reason to sell you a rebuild you do not need. Google checks the website behind your profile to confirm what you do and where, and a strong site makes every other piece of SEO work harder.

When the site genuinely is the problem, a thin one-pager that gives Google nothing to verify, we will show you why on the call. In that case the rebuild and the SEO become a single project instead of two separate bills, and we scope it so it makes sense as one move.

Which keywords do you actually target?

The ones your customers type in the last hour before they hire someone: your service plus their city, "near me" searches, and the cost and how-to questions specific to your trade. Those are the searches with real buying intent, and they are where a local business should spend its effort first.

We deliberately chase the terms with genuine intent and weak competition before the vanity terms, because ranking for a word nobody buys on is just decoration. The full method, step by step, is written up in our local SEO playbook, and the flat monthly rate that covers it is quoted on the free consultation.
