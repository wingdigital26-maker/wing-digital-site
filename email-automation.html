The service01 - What we do

Most jobs are not lost to a competitor with better work. They are lost to whoever replied first. A lead fills out your form at 7pm, hears nothing until Thursday, and by then they have already hired the company that texted them back in ninety seconds. We build the systems that make sure the fast reply is always yours, without you touching your phone.

What does email automation actually include?

An instant reply the moment someone fills out your form, so nobody sits in silence wondering if you are still in business. A follow-up sequence that keeps going after that, spaced out over days and weeks, because most people who ask for a quote are not ready to book the same afternoon. A review request that goes out automatically after a completed job. And a quiet nudge to old leads who went cold months ago.

Every message is written in your voice, not a template with your name pasted at the top. We write them once, wire them into your CRM, and they run from then on. You approve the wording before anything sends.

Why does speed matter this much?

Because a lead is at their hottest in the first few minutes and cools fast after that. Somebody researching a roofer at 9pm is comparing three companies in one sitting. The one that answers while they are still looking usually wins, and it often has nothing to do with price.

You cannot beat that clock manually while you are on a ladder or under a sink, which is exactly why it should be automated. The system does not get busy, forget, or go on vacation. It is the same reason we build the website to capture the lead cleanly in the first place, since automation only works if the form actually feeds it.

Does automated mean impersonal?

Only if it is written badly. The sequences we write sound like a person because we write them from how you actually talk, and they reference the specific thing the customer asked about. Most recipients never think twice about it, they just feel like they got a fast, straight answer.

The moment someone replies, the automation stops and it is a real conversation with you. The system exists to start conversations, not to fake them. If a sequence ever starts reading like spam, we rewrite it, and you always see the copy before it goes live.

How does this connect to reviews and repeat work?

The most valuable automation on the list is the one that runs after the job is done. A request for a review, sent automatically when work is marked complete, while the customer is still happy and standing in the finished room. Reviews are also one of the strongest signals in local search, so this quietly lifts your ranking too.

Then there is the long tail: seasonal reminders, maintenance nudges, and check-ins with customers from two years ago who forgot your name. That list of past customers is usually the most underused asset a local business has, and it is also where our lead generation work starts before we ever go looking for strangers.
