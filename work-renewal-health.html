Renewal Health

Website design for a functional health practice

Renewal Health is a functional health practice built around a root-cause approach, the kind of work that is easy to feel and hard to explain on a website. The goal was a site that felt as calm and considered as the practice itself, and that walked a visitor from "what is this" all the way to booking a consult without a single confusing step.

What we designed

We built the whole experience around the dragonfly brand mark and a soft, editorial palette. A full-screen hero opens the site with "Emerge. Align. Live Fully." over a quiet, moody image, and a short branded intro animation sets the tone before the page even settles. Serif headlines and generous white space make it read like a wellness magazine, not a clinic form.

Every section was written to explain functional health in plain language. Instead of leaning on medical jargon, the copy meets people where they are, describes the root-cause philosophy simply, and builds trust page by page toward the consultation.

What we built

A full multi-page site: home, services, programs and packages, an about page that tells the practitioner's story, a resources section, and a clear contact path. It is mobile-first, because most people find a health practice on their phone, and it is structured so a first-time visitor always knows the next step.

Under the hood it carries the same on-page SEO foundation we build into every website, so the practice can be found by the people searching for exactly this kind of care.

The result

A website that looks and feels like the practice: calm, credible, and clear. It gives Renewal Health a real online home that does the explaining, so conversations start with someone who already understands the approach and is ready to book.
