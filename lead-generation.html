The service01 - What we do

Referrals are the best leads you will ever get, and they are also the least predictable. They arrive in clusters, then go quiet for six weeks right when you need work. Lead generation is the part of the system that fills those gaps on purpose, so a slow month is something you saw coming instead of something that happened to you.

What does lead generation include?

We build a targeted list of the businesses or homeowners who actually fit what you do, in the cities you actually serve. Then we research them, so the outreach can reference something real instead of opening with a generic hello. Then we write and send the outreach in your voice, and we follow up on a schedule that keeps going without being obnoxious.

Everything that comes back lands in one place where you can see it: who replied, who is warm, who needs a call today. You are not digging through an inbox trying to remember which conversation was which.

How is this different from buying leads?

Bought leads are sold to four of your competitors at the same time, which is why they feel like a race to the bottom on price. The person on the other end is fielding four calls and picking the cheapest. You are not selling anymore, you are bidding.

What we build is your own pipeline, going to people nobody else in your trade is talking to that week, with your name on it. It takes longer to warm up and it is worth more, because you are the only one in the conversation. It also compounds: the list, the messaging, and the reply data are yours and get sharper every month.

How does this work with the rest of the system?

Lead generation is the front door, and it works best when everything behind it is ready. Outreach sends people to your website, so the site has to answer their questions and make it easy to reach you. Once they respond, our email automation handles the follow-up so nothing goes cold while you are on a job.

Meanwhile local SEO is pulling in the people already searching for you. One is you reaching out, the other is them finding you. Run both and the slow months stop being a surprise.

What do I have to do?

Tell us who your best customer is and answer the replies. That is genuinely it. We handle the list, the research, the writing, the sending, and the follow-up, and we flag anything that looks like a real conversation so it hits you the same day.

You will also tell us what is working, because you are the one on the calls. When you say a certain type of job is worth chasing and another is not, we adjust the targeting that week.
